Plain-language privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 August 2026

Soft Pace is designed to work without an account. Your check-ins, symptoms, preferences, custom activities, plans, activity progress and reflections are stored locally on your device.

Information handled by the app

Soft Pace lets you enter wellbeing information such as energy, sleep, mental clarity, symptoms, activity preferences and private reflections. This information is used on your device to suggest and track gentle activities.

This wellbeing information remains in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not send it to the developer, and the developer does not sell, rent or use it for advertising or tracking.

Local storage and backups

Your information remains in the app’s private storage on your device unless you choose Export backup. When you export a backup, you decide where the file is saved or shared. Any copy outside Soft Pace is controlled by the destination you select and is subject to that provider’s privacy practices.

Soft Pace does not upload your wellbeing information to a server, and it does not use iCloud Drive on its own. If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is switched on for your iPhone, iOS may include Soft Pace’s local data in that device backup. Deleting the app may remove its locally stored information unless you previously exported a backup or the data remains in an iPhone backup.

Data retention and deletion

Information remains on your device until you remove it. You can use Settings → Delete all data to delete Soft Pace data stored by the app. You can also delete the app from your device.

Because the developer does not receive your locally stored data, the developer cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Soft Pace 1.0 does not include third-party advertising, behavioural analytics, cross-app tracking or tracking technologies.

When you contact support

You decide what information to include when contacting support. Support emails are used only to respond to your request and are deleted from the developer’s mailbox when no longer reasonably needed. Do not send health information, private reflections or exported Soft Pace backup files.

Legal basis and privacy rights

Where data-protection law applies, support emails are processed to respond to your request and for the developer’s legitimate interests in providing support and maintaining the app’s security and reliability. If you have a privacy question or request concerning support correspondence, contact itotoro525@gmail.com. Where applicable, you also have the right to contact your local data-protection authority.

Children

Soft Pace is not directed to children and is not intended for the App Store Kids Category.

Wellbeing and medical safety

Soft Pace is a general wellbeing tool. It does not provide medical advice or diagnose, monitor, prevent or treat cancer or chemotherapy side effects, and it does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Choose only activities that are appropriate for you, and stop if an activity causes pain or makes you feel unwell. For medical questions, contact a qualified healthcare professional. For urgent, severe, sudden or rapidly worsening symptoms, contact local emergency services.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app, website or data practices change. Material changes will be communicated through the app, this website or the App Store listing before they take effect where reasonably possible. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about privacy or support can be sent to itotoro525@gmail.com. You can also visit the support page for help and current guidance.
