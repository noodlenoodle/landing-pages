Soft Pace Support

Answers to common questions about the app and your information.

Where is my information stored?

Soft Pace stores your information locally on your iPhone. The app does not require an account and does not upload your check-ins or plans to the developer. If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is switched on, iOS may include Soft Pace data in that device backup.

How do I make a backup?

Open Settings, select Export backup (JSON), and choose where to save the file. To restore one, select Import backup and choose a Soft Pace backup.

How do I delete my information?

Open Settings, select Delete all data, and confirm the deletion. This permanently removes the app’s preferences, custom activities, check-ins, plans and history from the device.

Is Soft Pace medical advice?

Soft Pace is a general wellbeing tool. It does not provide medical advice or diagnose, monitor, prevent or treat cancer or chemotherapy side effects, and it does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Choose only activities that are appropriate for you, and stop if an activity causes pain or makes you feel unwell. For medical questions, contact a qualified healthcare professional. For urgent, severe, sudden or rapidly worsening symptoms, contact local emergency services.
