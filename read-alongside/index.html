Build a reading habit.

Give reading a place in your day. Pick a time and setting you look forward to, choose a session length you can comfortably manage, and have your book ready when that moment arrives.

02 /

Make difficult books approachable.

Give challenging books a manageable place in your week. Rereading counts as reading, even when your page number stays the same. Reflect when it helps; writing is never required to log a session.

03 /

Make your reading time count.

Discover what makes reading enjoyable and rewarding for you. Reading Check-In helps you distinguish a worthwhile challenge from a poor fit, and make more room for books you want to spend time with.

Frequently asked questions

About Read Alongside

Is Read Alongside available yet?+

Read Alongside is coming soon for iOS. This page introduces the experience we’re building; download and launch details will be added here when they’re ready.

Do I need to read every day?+

No. Choose the reading days that suit your life. Missing a day doesn’t end your seven-day challenge, and you can adjust your routine whenever you need to.

Can I use Read Alongside with physical books?+

Yes. Read Alongside supports books you read on paper or elsewhere. It helps you plan, log, and reflect on your reading; it doesn’t provide ebook content.

What if I don’t finish a book?+

Finishing isn’t the only meaningful outcome. Reading Check-In lets you continue, give a book more time, pause, read selectively, or stop. Pausing or stopping keeps your reading history.

Will my reading be public?+

Your reading intentions and personal progress are private by default. Read our privacy policy for information about account storage, local drafts, and service providers. Visit the privacy page →
