Read Alongside Support

Updated 29 October 2026

Help with building a reading routine, making goals manageable, Reading Check-In, backups and optional iCloud Sync. Read Alongside is preparing for release for iPhone and iPad; some features may differ between beta builds.

Getting started

Add a book in Books, then create a reading plan in Goals. Choose your purpose, session duration, and reading schedule. An intention explaining why you chose the book is optional.

Saving and syncing between iPhone and iPad

Your books and reading history save automatically on the device. iCloud Sync is optional and is not required for local saving.

  1. On both iPhone and iPad, sign into iCloud in Apple Settings using the same Apple Account. Using the same App Store account alone is not enough.
  2. In Read Alongside on each device, open Settings → Data & Backup and turn on Sync with iCloud.
  3. Keep both devices online and open the app. Check the sync status in Data & Backup; you can also choose Sync now. Sync is not always instantaneous.

When enabled on both devices, your saved books, reading plans, current page positions, notes, confirmed sessions and check-ins can sync. Unfinished drafts, pending save requests and reminders stay on each device. Use a build that includes iCloud Sync on both devices.

When offline, keep reading and logging on the device. Sync retries while the app is open after a connection returns. Turning sync off does not delete the existing iCloud copy. Export a backup from Data & Backup if you want a separate copy in Files. Backup files include private notes and reading history and are not password protected; keep them somewhere you trust.

Logging reading and rereading

Use Log Reading to record a session. You can include pages or reading time when available. Completion-only sessions support reading or rereading without inventing page progress.

Changing your plan

Adjust your goal and reading routine using the existing plan and schedule controls. Restarting a planning period or changing a schedule preserves earlier reading history.

The seven-day challenge

The optional challenge uses your existing plan. Missing a session does not fail it. On Day 7, use Reading Check-In to reflect and decide what to do next; finishing the book is not required.

Reading Check-In

Use Reading Check-In to reflect on writing and fit, attention, and how reading feels. Review a neutral summary, then choose to keep reading, give it more time, pause, read selectively, or mark it DNF (Did Not Finish). No choice is preselected or inferred from your answers. The app previews the consequences before you confirm. Pausing or stopping preserves your reading history. Check-in is optional and does not block saving a reading session.

Weekly review

The review summarizes the previous completed calendar week in your local time. Reading days count once even if you logged several sessions. Pages appear only when recorded page data is available.

Missing records or interrupted saves

If records are missing on another device, check that Sync with iCloud is enabled on both devices, both use the same iCloud Apple Account, and both have an internet connection. Check the sync status in Data & Backup. A pending session is not confirmed reading history; pending requests and drafts stay on the device until resolved. Do not reinstall the app while trying to recover unsaved records. Contact support before clearing local data.

Reminders and book scanning

Review your reading days, time, and reminder preferences, then check Read Alongside’s notification permission in iPhone or iPad Settings. For scanning, allow camera access or enter the book manually. Book lookup uses Google Books with an Open Library fallback, requires a connection, and may not find every edition. You can enter the book manually if lookup is unavailable.

Deleting your data

There is no app account to delete. In Settings → Data & Backup, Delete local data permanently removes this device’s library, notes, drafts, reminders and local recovery copies, and turns sync off on this device. It does not remove your iCloud library or exported backups.

Use the separate Delete iCloud copy action in the iCloud Sync section to remove the active cloud library for your Apple Account. This leaves local libraries and exported backups intact. Other devices require reconnection before syncing again. Turning sync off or deleting the app alone does not delete the cloud library.

Earlier beta builds used Firebase. That integration was removed from the app on 29 October 2026; the current app cannot access or delete records left in that former service. Contact support if you used an earlier account-based beta and need help.
