Read Alongside Support

Updated 29 October 2026

Help with building a reading routine, making goals manageable, Reading Check-In, backups and optional iCloud Sync. Read Alongside is preparing for release for iPhone and iPad; some features may differ between beta builds.

Getting started

In Books, add a book by entering or scanning its ISBN, or use Manual Entry with a title and total pages. You can keep it on your shelf or create a reading plan. In Goals, choose the book, your purpose and a finish date. A note about why you chose the book is optional. Set reading days, a time and a session length in your routine; reminders are optional.

Saving and syncing between iPhone and iPad

Your books and reading history save automatically on the device. iCloud Sync is optional and is not required for local saving.

  1. On both iPhone and iPad, sign into iCloud in Apple Settings using the same Apple Account. Using the same App Store account alone is not enough.
  2. In Read Alongside on each device, open Settings → Data & Backup and turn on Sync with iCloud.
  3. Keep both devices online and open the app. Check the sync status in Data & Backup; you can also choose Sync now. Sync is not always instantaneous.

When enabled on both devices, your saved books, reading plans, current page positions, notes, confirmed sessions and check-ins can sync. Unfinished drafts, pending save requests and reminders stay on each device. Use a build that includes iCloud Sync on both devices.

When offline, keep reading and logging on the device. Sync retries while the app is open after a connection returns. Turning sync off does not delete the existing iCloud copy. Export a backup from Data & Backup if you want a separate copy in Files. Backup files include private notes and reading history and are not password protected; keep them somewhere you trust.

Logging reading and rereading

Use Log Reading to record a session. You can include pages or reading time when available. Completion-only sessions support reading or rereading without inventing page progress.

Changing your plan

Your reading plan uses a finish date to calculate page targets. Monthly and quarterly options propose one or three calendar months; you can also choose a date or use the pages-per-day calculator to calculate one. The calculator sets a finish date—it does not create a separate fixed-pace plan. Your routine’s weekdays and duration reserve reading opportunities; they do not make the page calculation count only those weekdays.

Completing today’s page target and being on schedule overall are different. If you need more time, review and edit your finish date. Changing a date or correcting your current page does not create a reading session. Restarting the planning period preserves earlier history.

Exporting and importing a backup

Open Settings → Data & Backup → Export backup and choose a location in Files. To restore a supported Read Alongside JSON backup, choose Import backup, review its contents, and confirm Merge backup. Identical records are skipped. Changed versions of the same record are rejected rather than silently replacing your current data. If an import cannot be merged, keep both files and contact support before deleting your library.

Exported files include private notes and history and are not password protected. Store and share them carefully. Exported files and device backups are separate from the active iCloud library.

When iCloud asks you to review changes

If Data & Backup shows Review changes, compare This device with iCloud. Choose Keep this device’s library or Use iCloud library only after reviewing the versions. This chooses an active library; it does not add page positions together. Both versions are retained as recovery copies, which you can export under Recovery backups.

If the Apple Account has changed, verify the account in device Settings before choosing Connect this library. Connecting can upload the local library or reload that account’s cloud library. You do not need to share your Apple Account password with support.

The seven-day challenge

The optional challenge uses your existing plan. Missing a session does not fail it. On Day 7, use Reading Check-In to reflect and decide what to do next; finishing the book is not required.

Reading Check-In

Use Reading Check-In to reflect on writing and fit, attention, and how reading feels. Review a neutral summary, then choose to keep reading, give it more time, pause, read selectively, or mark it DNF (Did Not Finish). No choice is preselected or inferred from your answers. The app previews the consequences before you confirm. Pausing or stopping preserves your reading history. Check-in is optional and does not block saving a reading session.

Putting a book on hold or marking it DNF

From an eligible book’s library menu, choose Put on Hold or Mark as DNF. These actions do not require a check-in. Read the explanation and confirm, or cancel without changing anything. DNF means Did Not Finish: the unfinished plan becomes inactive, while the book, current page, notes and history remain. Use the book details to resume when you are ready.

To correct an entered page position, use the book’s progress correction controls. To change what a saved session records, edit that session. A correction is not a new reading session.

Weekly review

The review summarizes the previous completed calendar week in your local time. Reading days count once even if you logged several sessions. Pages appear only when recorded page data is available.

Missing records or interrupted saves

If records are missing on another device, check that Sync with iCloud is enabled on both devices, both use the same iCloud Apple Account, and both have an internet connection. Check the sync status in Data & Backup. If it asks you to review versions, follow the review-changes instructions. A pending session is not confirmed reading history; pending requests and drafts stay on the device until resolved. Do not reinstall the app while trying to recover unsaved records. Contact support before clearing local data.

If a form will not save

After an unsuccessful Add, Next or Save, read the reminder and the highlighted field’s explanation. Correct that field and try again; the app retains your input. Optional notes, intentions and authors do not become required because another field is invalid. A network or storage error is different from missing information—keep the draft and follow the message shown.

Reminders and book scanning

Review your reading days, time, and reminder preferences, then check Read Alongside’s notification permission in iPhone or iPad Settings. For scanning, allow camera access or enter the book manually. Book lookup uses Google Books with an Open Library fallback, requires a connection, and may not find every edition. You can enter the book manually if lookup is unavailable.

Deleting your data

There is no app account to delete. In Settings → Data & Backup, Delete local data permanently removes this device’s library, notes, drafts, reminders and local recovery copies, and turns sync off on this device. It does not remove your iCloud library or exported backups.

Use the separate Delete iCloud copy action in the iCloud Sync section to remove the active cloud library for your Apple Account. This leaves local libraries and exported backups intact. Other devices require reconnection before syncing again. Turning sync off or deleting the app alone does not delete the cloud library.

Earlier beta builds used Firebase. That integration was removed from the app on 29 October 2026; the current app cannot access or delete records left in that former service. Contact support if you used an earlier account-based beta and need help.
