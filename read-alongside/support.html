Read Alongside Support

Updated 14 October 2026

Help with reading plans, progress, and Reading Check-In. Read Alongside is currently preparing for release; some features may differ between beta builds.

Contact support

For help, feedback, or privacy requests:

Email support ↗

appsbyjing@gmail.com

Please include your app version, iOS version, device model, and the steps that led to the issue. Never include your password. Hide private reading notes in screenshots.

Getting started

Add a book in Books, then create a reading plan in Goals. Choose your purpose, session duration, and reading schedule. An intention explaining why you chose the book is optional.

Logging reading and rereading

Use Log Reading to record a session. You can include pages or reading time when available. Completion-only sessions support reading or rereading without inventing page progress.

Changing your plan

Adjust your goal and reading routine using the existing plan and schedule controls. Restarting a planning period or changing a schedule preserves earlier reading history.

The seven-day challenge

The optional challenge uses your existing plan. Missing a session does not fail it. On Day 7, use Reading Check-In to reflect and decide what to do next; finishing the book is not required.

Reading Check-In

Open a book’s check-in to reflect on your original intention and current experience. Choose to keep reading, give it more time, pause, read selectively, or stop. The decision is yours, and pausing or stopping preserves your reading history.

Weekly review

The review summarizes the previous completed calendar week in your local time. Reading days count once even if you logged several sessions. Pages appear only when recorded page data is available.

Missing records or interrupted saves

Check that you are signed in to the same account and that your device has an internet connection. Pending sessions and drafts can remain on the device until a save completes. Do not reinstall the app while trying to recover unsaved records. Contact support before clearing local data.

Reminders and book scanning

Review your reading days, time, and reminder preferences, then check Read Alongside’s notification permission in iOS Settings. For scanning, allow camera access or enter the book manually. Book lookup requires a connection and may not find every edition.

Account and data deletion

In-app deletion is being validated before release. If your build does not have a working Delete Account option, email appsbyjing@gmail.com from your account email to request help. Do not send a password. Deleting the app alone does not delete your cloud account or reading records.

Privacy

Learn what is stored on your device, what is stored using Firebase, and how book lookup works in the Privacy Policy.
