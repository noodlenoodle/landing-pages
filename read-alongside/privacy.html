Read Alongside · privacy

Privacy Policy

Updated 14 October 2026

Read Alongside helps you plan and reflect on your reading. Your account-backed reading records are stored using Firebase; reading routines and unfinished drafts are also stored on your device.

Who is responsible?

For privacy questions or requests, email appsbyjing@gmail.com.

Information the app handles

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, and display name if provided. Firebase Authentication handles your sign-in credentials.
  • Your reading records: books you add, reading position, goals, session dates, pages and optional reading time, intentions, notes, check-in answers, and decisions.
  • Device-local information: reading days, preferred time, reminders, appearance preferences, unfinished setup and check-in drafts, and pending reading sessions.
  • Support correspondence: your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send.

How information is used

Account and reading information is used to sign you in, save your library and progress, calculate your plan, display reading reviews, and return you to your earlier records. Your check-in answers support your own reading decisions; they are not public book ratings.

Personal reading intentions and progress are private by default. The app does not automatically publish them.

Storage and service providers

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, provided by Google, handle account access and cloud storage. Service providers may process technical information needed to operate and secure their services. Data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. See Firebase’s privacy and security information.

Reading routines and drafts are account-separated on the device. Not all device-local preferences synchronize between devices. The Firebase SDK may cache cloud records on the device. Device or computer backups may contain local app information.

Book lookup and camera access

When you look up a book by ISBN, the ISBN is sent to Google Books to retrieve book details. Loading a book cover can also contact its image provider. These requests expose ordinary network information, such as your IP address, to the receiving service.

Camera access is optional and is used to scan book barcodes. You can add books manually instead. The barcode-scanning workflow does not upload camera images to Read Alongside’s database.

Analytics and diagnostics

The current app includes the Firebase Analytics SDK. Google may collect app-interaction and technical information through its automatic SDK behavior, such as app version, device information, and usage events. Read Alongside does not add reading notes or intentions as custom analytics event parameters in the implementation reviewed for this policy.

TestFlight and Apple diagnostic services may separately process beta feedback, crash reports, or usage information under Apple’s settings and privacy terms. This policy does not describe those services as anonymous.

Permissions and your choices

You can manage camera and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Reading reminders are optional. You may skip optional notes and intentions, change your reading plan, and decide whether to continue, pause, or stop a book.

Retention and deletion

Account-backed records remain until they are removed through an available app function or an account-deletion request is completed. Removing the app alone does not delete your Firebase account or cloud records. Copies in device backups and information already processed by other services may remain under those services’ retention practices.

Current pre-release status: in-app account deletion is being validated and its production backend is not yet confirmed available. Until that release is ready, contact appsbyjing@gmail.com to request account or data deletion. Identity verification may be needed. Do not send your password.

Deletion is intended to remove your personal library, goals, reading history, intentions, and check-ins. It does not remove other readers’ records or copies you have saved elsewhere. A minimal account-deletion identifier may be retained to prevent stale credentials from recreating deleted data.

Support emails

Support messages are used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the problem you describe. Email providers process your message to deliver it. Read Alongside does not automatically attach your reading records. Please avoid sending passwords, private notes, or unnecessary personal information. Correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to handle support, security, or legal matters.

Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your personal data, and may have rights to restrict or object to processing. Contact us at appsbyjing@gmail.com. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Where applicable, account and reading data are processed to provide the service you request. Support and security processing serve the legitimate interests of responding to users and maintaining a reliable service. Optional permissions remain under your control.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the app’s practices change. The date above identifies this version. Questions can be sent to appsbyjing@gmail.com.
