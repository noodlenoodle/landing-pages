Privacy Policy

Updated 24 November 2026

Contact And Scope

For privacy questions or requests concerning Read Alongside, contact appsbyjing@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Read Alongside app and its marketing, support and privacy pages.

Information The App Handles

Read Alongside works without an app account. Your reading library saves on your device.

The app handles:

• Books you enter, reading positions and goals.

• Session dates, pages and optional reading minutes.

• Reading intentions, notes, check-in answers and decisions.

• Reading routines, reminders, unfinished drafts and pending reading sessions.

This information saves your library and progress, calculates your plan, displays reading reviews and lets you return to earlier records. Check-in answers support your own decisions; they do not automatically pause or stop a book.

Your reading information is not automatically published. The developer does not operate a server containing your reading library and cannot remotely inspect it. Support can inspect only information you choose to send.

Local Storage And Optional Icloud Sync

Reading information saves locally without enabling iCloud Sync.

To synchronize compatible iPhone and iPad devices, enable Settings → Data & Backup → Sync with iCloud on each device and use the same Apple Account. Internet access is required to transfer changes.

When enabled, saved books and metadata, reading plans, positions, notes, confirmed sessions, check-ins, preserved history and removal records are synchronized through your private CloudKit database, operated by Apple.

Unfinished drafts, pending reading requests and reminder preferences remain on each device. The app uses an account identifier locally to detect account changes; it does not receive your Apple Account password.

Local reading remains available offline. Sync retries while the app is active when access returns; delivery may not be immediate. Turning sync off stops updates on that device but does not delete the existing cloud copy.

If library versions cannot be combined safely, the app retains both as local recovery copies and asks which active library to continue with. Page positions are not added together. Changing the Apple Account can require confirmation before connecting your local library to that account.

Apple’s services are subject to its applicable terms and privacy information:

apple.com/legal/privacy

Backups

You can export and import backups in Settings → Data & Backup.

Exports may contain books, plans, sessions, private notes, check-ins, device-local preferences and recoverable drafts. Exported files are not password protected by the app. Choose their storage location and recipients carefully.

Files saved in Files or iCloud Drive, device backups and local recovery copies are separate from the active cloud library.

Import validates supported backups and merges nonconflicting records. It does not silently replace conflicting records. A local pre-import recovery copy is retained.

Book Entry, Search And Artwork

The current app uses manual book entry: title, optional authors and total pages. Searching saved titles and authors happens locally.

The app does not offer ISBN catalog lookup, barcode scanning or remote book search. It uses book-cover placeholders and does not download cover artwork. Older saved or imported records may retain external artwork links as metadata.

Analytics And Diagnostics

The current app contains no analytics SDK, does not send usage analytics and does not contact catalog or cover-art providers. It does not send reading records to an AI service.

Apple’s TestFlight and diagnostic services may separately process beta feedback, crash reports or usage information under Apple’s settings and privacy terms. These services are not described here as anonymous.

Support Emails

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. The app does not automatically attach reading records.

Correspondence is used to answer your request, investigate reported problems and handle privacy requests. We rely on our legitimate interests in providing support and maintaining a reliable, secure service. Where particular processing is required by law, we rely on that legal obligation.

Please avoid sending passwords, private reading notes or unnecessary personal information.

We review resolved support requests monthly and delete routine correspondence and attachments from the support mailbox no later than 12 months after resolution. This includes received messages, sent replies and copies downloaded for support under our control. We empty the relevant Trash entries as part of deletion; archiving is not deletion.

Relevant information may be retained longer only where necessary for an unresolved dispute, security investigation or legal obligation. Longer retention is limited to the information needed, reviewed when the matter ends and deleted when the reason no longer applies.

Our deletion routine covers copies under our control. It does not delete copies held by the sender. Google may retain residual copies for limited periods under its deletion, backup, security and legal-retention processes; mailbox deletion does not guarantee immediate removal from every provider system.

Retention And Deletion

Reading records remain in the active local library until you change or delete them.

Removing a book preserves its recorded reading history. Its library entry and plan are removed, while its title, original session identifiers, reading dates, accepted pages, recorded minutes and milestones remain for history and reviews. Minutes are not inferred from pages. Book removal is not full data erasure.

Delete local data in Settings → Data & Backup removes the local library, notes, drafts, reminders and local recovery copies, and turns sync off on that device. It does not delete the iCloud library, exported files or device backups.

Delete iCloud copy removes the app’s active cloud library for the current Apple Account and turns sync off on that device. Local libraries, exported files and recovery copies remain separate.

Uninstalling the app or turning sync off does not itself delete the cloud copy. Copies on other devices, exported backups and device backups must be managed separately.

Your Choices And Rights

You can use the app without enabling iCloud Sync or notifications. Notes and reading minutes are optional. Notification permissions can be changed in your device settings.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information we hold. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Contact appsbyjing@gmail.com. We normally respond within one month, subject to extensions permitted by applicable law. We may request proportionate information to verify a request.

We cannot retrieve a reading library held only on your device or in your private iCloud database. The app’s export and deletion controls let you manage those records directly.

You may lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. In Sweden, this is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY):
