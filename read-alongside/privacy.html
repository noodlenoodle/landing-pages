Privacy Policy

Updated 29 October 2026

Read Alongside helps you plan and reflect on your reading. In the current local-first build, your reading records save on your device without an app account. Optional iCloud Sync lets you keep a saved library across your iPhone and iPad. The app has no login, no app account, no server of its own and no analytics SDK. Earlier beta data is addressed separately below.

Who is responsible?

For privacy questions or requests, email appsbyjing@gmail.com.

Information the app handles

  • Your reading records: books you add, reading position, goals, session dates, pages and optional reading time, intentions, notes, check-in answers, and decisions.
  • Device-local information: reading days, preferred time, reminders, appearance preferences, unfinished setup and check-in drafts, and pending reading sessions.
  • Support correspondence: your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send.

How information is used

Reading information is used to save your library and progress, calculate your plan, display reading reviews, and return you to your earlier records. Your check-in answers are saved with your reading records and support your own decisions. They are not public book ratings and are not used to automatically pause or stop a book.

Personal reading intentions and progress are private by default. The app does not automatically publish them.

Local storage and optional iCloud Sync

Reading data saves automatically on your device. You do not need to enable iCloud Sync to save locally. To synchronize between iPhone and iPad, enable Settings → Data & Backup → Sync with iCloud in the app on each device, and use the same Apple Account signed into iCloud. Both devices need a compatible build and internet access to transfer changes.

When enabled, the app sends saved books and metadata, reading plans, current page positions, notes, confirmed reading sessions and check-ins to your private CloudKit database, operated by Apple. Unfinished drafts, pending requests and reminder preferences stay on each device. The app uses an account identifier locally to detect account changes; it does not receive your Apple Account password. See Apple’s privacy information.

Local reading remains available offline. Sync retries while the app is active when access returns; delivery is not guaranteed to be immediate. Turning sync off stops updates on that device but does not delete the existing cloud copy. If library versions cannot be combined safely, the app retains both as local recovery backups and asks you which active library to continue with. Page positions are not added together. Changing the Apple Account can require confirmation before connecting the local library to that account.

Exported files in Files or iCloud Drive, device backups and local recovery copies are separate from the active cloud library. Exported backups can include books, plans, sessions, private notes, check-ins and device-local preferences or recoverable drafts. They are not password protected; choose their storage and recipients carefully. Import validates a supported backup and merges nonconflicting records; it does not silently replace conflicting records. A local pre-import recovery copy is retained. See sync setup and troubleshooting.

Previous Firebase account data

Earlier beta builds used Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Firebase, its login and recovery flow, and its analytics integration were removed from the app on 29 October 2026. The current app cannot read or delete records left in that former service. Deleting the current local or iCloud library does not remove earlier beta records. Contact support if you used an earlier account-based beta and need help with that data.

Book lookup and camera access

When you look up a book by ISBN, the ISBN is sent to Google Books to retrieve matching edition details, with Open Library used as a fallback when needed. Searching the books already in your library by title or author is local. The current Add Book flow uses ISBN lookup or manual entry; it does not offer a remote title search. Loading a book cover can also contact its image provider. These requests expose ordinary network information, such as your IP address, to the receiving service.

Camera access is optional and is used to scan book barcodes. You can add books manually instead. Barcode scanning processes the camera image on the device; the detected ISBN is used for catalog lookup. The app has no server database to receive camera images.

Analytics and diagnostics

The current app contains no analytics SDK and does not send usage analytics. Earlier beta builds may have used Firebase Analytics; it is no longer part of the app. Catalog and artwork providers still receive ordinary network information when contacted; local or private-cloud storage does not mean all third-party requests are anonymous.

TestFlight and Apple diagnostic services may separately process beta feedback, crash reports, or usage information under Apple’s settings and privacy terms. This policy does not describe those services as anonymous.

Permissions and your choices

You can manage camera and notification permissions in iPhone or iPad Settings. Reading reminders are optional. You may skip optional notes and intentions, change your reading plan, and decide whether to continue, pause, or stop a book.

Retention and deletion

In Data & Backup, Delete local data removes the local library, notes, drafts, reminders and local recovery copies, and turns iCloud Sync off on that device. It does not delete the iCloud library, exported files, device backups or previous Firebase account data.

Delete iCloud copy removes the app’s active cloud library for the current Apple Account and turns sync off here. Local libraries, exported backups and recovery copies remain separate. Other devices require reconnection before syncing again. Turning sync off or uninstalling the app alone does not delete cloud records.

Earlier beta data: the current app has no account-deletion service and cannot access the former Firebase project. Contact appsbyjing@gmail.com for help with older account or data deletion. Identity verification may be needed; do not send your password. Copies already saved elsewhere or processed by other services may remain under those services’ retention practices.

Support emails

Support messages are used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the problem you describe. Email providers process your message to deliver it. Read Alongside does not automatically attach your reading records. Please avoid sending passwords, private notes, or unnecessary personal information. Correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to handle support, security, or legal matters.

Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your personal data, and may have rights to restrict or object to processing. Contact us at appsbyjing@gmail.com. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

The app manages your reading library on your device and, if you enable it, in your private iCloud database. The developer does not have an app account or developer-operated database through which to access that library. Support can only inspect reading information you choose to send. Optional camera and notification permissions remain under your control.

This website

The marketing, support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them makes a request to GitHub’s hosting infrastructure, which receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address. This site does not add analytics scripts, advertising, a sign-in form or a mailing-list form. The check-in choice explorer runs in your browser and does not save or transmit your selection.

See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for its hosting practices. Following an email link opens your email application; a message is sent only if you send it.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the app’s practices change. The date above identifies this version. Questions can be sent to appsbyjing@gmail.com.
