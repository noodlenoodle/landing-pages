DESCRIPTION (2776/4000 characters)
Start small. Find your pace. Read with intention.

Build a reading habit with manageable goals. When a book gets difficult, take a moment to reflect: is the challenge worthwhile, or does this book no longer fit your interests and goals? Choose your next step without pressure to finish.

START SMALL. MAKE TIME.
Begin with a few pages or a short reading session. Choose a familiar time and comfortable place, keep your book ready, and build a routine you can return to. Optional reminders support the reading days you choose.

MAKE CHALLENGING BOOKS MANAGEABLE
A long book, unfamiliar topic or demanding writing can make it difficult to keep going—even when you care about what you’re reading.

Choose a finish date and see the daily pages needed to reach it. Use the pages-per-day calculator to find a manageable date, then adjust your plan as your time and energy change. Rereading counts too: spending time with a difficult passage can matter even when your page number stays the same.

REFLECT BEFORE DECIDING WHAT’S NEXT
Your reading time is limited. Sometimes a book asks for patience; sometimes its style, subject or timing doesn’t fit what you need. Time already spent does not have to determine what you read next.

Reading Check-In helps you notice what is and isn’t clicking, whether your attention is drifting, and how reading feels. Review a neutral summary of your answers, then consider: is this a worthwhile challenge? If you were choosing today, would you choose this book again?

Choose your next step: keep reading, give it more time, pause, read selectively, or stop. No choice is preselected or inferred from your answers. Review what your choice means before confirming it. Your reading history is preserved. You can also put a book on hold or mark it DNF (Did Not Finish) from its library menu without completing a check-in.

BUILD A HABIT YOU WANT TO RETURN TO
Record pages, optional reading time, or a completed reading session. Keep optional notes and know where to pick up again. Look back at your previous week to understand what fits your life.

Try an optional seven-day challenge to explore a routine. Missing a session doesn’t end it, and finishing the book isn’t required.

YOUR BOOKS. YOUR READING LIFE.
Start without creating an app account. Your library saves on your device. Optional iCloud Sync uses your private iCloud library with the same Apple Account on your devices. Export and import backups in Settings → Data & Backup.

Read Alongside supports books you read on paper or elsewhere. It does not provide ebooks or audiobooks.
