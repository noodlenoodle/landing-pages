Information handled by the app

Original

Soft Pace lets you enter wellbeing information such as energy, sleep, mental clarity, symptoms, activity preferences and private reflections. This information is used on your device to suggest and track gentle activities.

The developer does not receive, collect, sell, rent or use this information for advertising or tracking.

Revised

Soft Pace lets you enter wellbeing information such as energy, sleep, mental clarity, symptoms, activity preferences and private reflections. This information is used on your device to suggest and track gentle activities.

This wellbeing information remains in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not send it to the developer, and the developer does not sell, rent or use it for advertising or tracking.

Support correspondence

Approved: final shortened version

Original

No explanation of information received by email, Gmail processing or support retention.

Revised

You decide what information to include when contacting support. Support emails are used only to respond to your request and are deleted from the developer’s mailbox when no longer reasonably needed. Do not send health information, private reflections or exported Soft Pace backup files.

Legal basis and privacy rights

Approved: final revised version

Original

No legal-basis, individual-rights or regulator section.

Revised

Where data-protection law applies, support emails are processed to respond to your request and for the developer’s legitimate interests in providing support and maintaining the app’s security and reliability. If you have a privacy question or request concerning support correspondence, contact appsbyjing@gmail.com. Where applicable, you also have the right to contact your local data-protection authority.

Medical-safety disclaimer

Approved: revised version

Original

Soft Pace provides general wellbeing and activity-planning tools. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, monitoring or treatment. For medical questions or decisions, contact a qualified healthcare professional. For urgent or severe symptoms, contact local emergency services.

Revised

Soft Pace is a general wellbeing tool. It does not provide medical advice or diagnose, monitor, prevent or treat cancer or chemotherapy side effects, and it does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Choose only activities that are appropriate for you, and stop if an activity causes pain or makes you feel unwell. For medical questions, contact a qualified healthcare professional. For urgent, severe, sudden or rapidly worsening symptoms, contact local emergency services.

Contacting support

Approved: revised version

Original

Email appsbyjing@gmail.com. When contacting support, include your iPhone model, iOS version, Soft Pace version and a short description of what happened. Do not send private health information or an exported backup unless support specifically requests it and you understand the privacy implications.

Revised

Email appsbyjing@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Soft Pace version and a short description of what happened.

Do not email health information, private reflections or an exported Soft Pace backup. These materials are not needed for ordinary support.

Your email address, message and the technical details you provide are used to respond to your request. Read the privacy policy for information about support correspondence.

Unchanged: Local storage; backup export and import; deleting app information; Support email; Privacy Policy link.
